Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

JOBSMETRIX ("we," "our," or "us") is operated by TDX Systems & Consulting LLC. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our construction management platform at jobsmetrix.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name and email address (via Google account sign-in)
  • Cell phone number (entered voluntarily in team profile settings)
  • Job and project information entered into the platform
  • Messages sent through the platform and customer portal
  • Daily logs, time entries, and work records

2. SMS / Text Message Communications

JOBSMETRIX sends SMS notifications to team members and crew leads who have provided their cell phone number in their profile settings. By entering your phone number in the platform, you consent to receive text message notifications from JOBSMETRIX.

SMS notifications may include:

  • New customer messages received through the customer portal
  • @mention alerts when you are mentioned in a job communication
  • Job status updates and action required alerts

Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on job activity. You may receive multiple messages per day during active job periods.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-out: You may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message from JOBSMETRIX. You may also remove your phone number from your team profile settings within the platform.

Help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at travis@jtxdgroup.com.

3. Mobile Number Non-Sharing Policy

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your mobile phone number to any third party for marketing purposes. Mobile phone numbers collected by JOBSMETRIX are used solely to deliver operational SMS notifications related to your activity within the platform. Your phone number is never used for advertising or shared with partners, advertisers, or data brokers.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and improve the JOBSMETRIX platform
  • Send operational SMS notifications as described above
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore) on Google Cloud infrastructure. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

6. Third-Party Services

JOBSMETRIX uses the following third-party services to operate the platform:

  • Google Firebase — database and authentication
  • Twilio — SMS message delivery
  • SendGrid — email delivery
  • QuickBooks Online — accounting integration (optional)
  • Google Calendar — calendar sync (optional)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing their handling of data.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at travis@jtxdgroup.com. Team members may also remove their phone number directly from their profile settings within the platform at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised date.
